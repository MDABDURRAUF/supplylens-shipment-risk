Model report: shipment delay and delivery risk

SupplyLens Shipment Risk

On 4,068 shipments tendered after training ended, the model separates late from on-time shipments with a ROC AUC of 0.863 and removes 27% of the error of always quoting the average late rate. The riskiest tenth of shipments contains 37% of all late deliveries. Its median delivery-date prediction is off by 0.74 days on average, against 1.02 days for trusting the carrier's promised date.

Data and split

Trained on 18,804 shipments delivered before 20 Oct 2025; probabilities and delivery-date confidence levels calibrated on 3,887 later shipments; tested on 4,068 shipments tendered from 13 Feb 2026. Splits are by time, and a shipment is used for training only if it had been delivered before the next period began.

Limits

Accuracy is measured on one held-out period of the data supplied; it can change when carriers, lanes or conditions change, so refit regularly and watch the drift report. The model predicts from patterns in past shipments and knows nothing about events absent from that history. Predictions for carriers or lanes with little history lean on averages.
